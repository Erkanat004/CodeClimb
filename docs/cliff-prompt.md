# Background asset

Tool: built-in ImageGen. Output: `public/cliff.png`.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-screen background for a mountain-climbing programming game, landscape 1536x1024. Primary request: create an original cinematic photograph of an enormous sheer granite monolith, like El Capitan, seen front-on from the valley. The very broad vertical rock face dominates the CENTER and right 80 percent of the image from top to bottom, with its slightly rounded flat summit visible near the top 12 percent. A smaller distant cliff on the far left and tiny dark pine trees at the bottom give scale. Strong richly saturated deep petrol blue and cyan duotone color grading; pale cool cyan sky at top, detailed vertical striations and stone cracks, atmospheric navy shadows at the base, restrained cinematic contrast. The rock face should have relatively even mid-blue luminance so a white thin route diagram will be readable when overlaid by code. Natural rock photograph, not illustration, not polygonal mountains, not futuristic buildings. Absolutely no route lines, no text, no numbers, no people, no avatars, no logos, no frames, no UI, no watermarks. This is a clean standalone image texture/background, not a screenshot.
